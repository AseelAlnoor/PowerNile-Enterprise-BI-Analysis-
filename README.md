# PowerNile Enterprise Performance & Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview & Business Context
An end-to-end business intelligence and data analytics project evaluating the operational and financial performance of **PowerNile**, a multi-sector enterprise operating across **Mining, Fuel Stations, Solar Energy, and Real Estate**. 

The primary objective was to provide executive management with a unified, data-driven framework to monitor revenue streams, track project budget execution risks, and optimize human resource allocation across 9 interconnected operational domains.

## Methodology & Tech Stack
* **Excel:** Initial data exploration, structural validation, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP data enrichment, PivotTables, and preliminary modeling.
* **SQL (SQLite):** Advanced data querying utilizing Complex Joins, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), and Window Functions (`RANK()`, `SUM() OVER()`, `LAG()`) to extract deep operational insights.
* **PowerBI:** Enterprise-grade relational data modeling, DAX measures (KPIs, Financial Health metrics, Target vs. Actual comparisons), interactive dashboards, and drill-through reporting.

## Key Findings & Strategic Insights
1. **Revenue Performance:** Total enterprise revenue reached **$132.18M**, surpassing the baseline target of **$61M** by **116%**, heavily driven by fuel operations and strong product diversification in the solar sector[cite: 1, 4, 5].
2. **Project Risk Management:** Budget execution tracking revealed that critical operational projects (such as heavy equipment safety programs and solar station maintenance) operate with high efficiency ($\ge 90\%$ budget utilization), while highlighting proactive risk warnings for over-budget or stalled initiatives[cite: 3].
3. **Workforce & Payroll Optimization:** Cross-departmental salary analysis and employee project allocation identified workload bottlenecks and optimized payroll distribution across technical and operational divisions[cite: 3, 5].
